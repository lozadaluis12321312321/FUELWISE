02

How the brain decides.

  1. 1

    Tracks your tank

    You log fill-ups and odometer readings. FuelWise learns your real consumption — not the brochure number.

  2. 2

    Watches for "low"

    When your estimated range dips under your threshold, the decision engine wakes up. No tap required.

  3. 3

    Scores every station

    For each saved station: fuel price × litres needed, plus the fuel cost of the detour, plus the value of your time.

  4. 4

    Picks the true winner

    One recommendation, one reason, in plain language. Can't reach the far one safely? It tells you that too.

true_cost = (price × litres) + (detour_km ÷ efficiency × price) + (detour_min × your_hourly_value)

The whole model fits in a few kilobytes. That's why it runs with airplane mode on.
